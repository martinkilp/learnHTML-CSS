## Understand the Core Anatomy of a Grid:

    * Margins: The outer blank boundaries that frame your content area and give the design breathing room.
    * Columns: The vertical blocks where your content (text and images) is placed.
    * Gutters / Alleys: The empty space or gaps separating your columns and rows, used to keep elements distinct.
    * Flow Lines: Horizontal alignment lines that guide where elements sit vertically across the page. 
  
## Choose the Right Type of Grid:

    * Manuscript Grid: A simple, single-column block used for traditional books and heavy text documents.
    * Column Grid: A vertical multi-section setup (often 12 columns) ideal for magazines, newspapers, and websites.
    * Modular Grid: Combines columns and rows to form small rectangular "modules," perfect for e-commerce or complex layouts.
    * Baseline Grid: A dense set of horizontal lines that align lines of body text and typography cleanly across columns
